Porto. Born in Portugal in 1981, he holds a degree in Accounting and has a lifelong passion for sport, particularly cycling and, more recently, surfing. His idea to create a cycling holiday company emerged from a bicycle journey to **Santiago de Compostela**, which changed his view on travel and tourism. Sérgio is directly involved in **route design, logistics, and tour planning**, covering regions such as the **Portuguese Camino, Douro Valley, Algarve, and Portugal’s Atlantic Coast**. He manages the company’s day‑to‑day operations and occasionally works as a guide, especially for larger groups. Sérgio speaks **Portuguese and English**.
