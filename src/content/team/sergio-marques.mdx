Sergio, the founder of Top Bike Tours Portugal, was born in Portugal in 1981. He holds a degree in Accounting and has a great passion for sports, particularly cycling and, more recently, surfing. Sergio has played both roller hockey and volleyball. His idea to create a cycling holiday company emerged from a journey he undertook by bicycle with friends to Santiago de Compostela. Sergio's main goal is to share his passion for cycling with his customers, reflecting his unique perspective on the tourism industry. His responsibilities include managing the company, and he occasionally serves as a guide, especially for large groups that can be challenging to manage, which he enjoys. Sergio speaks both Portuguese and English.
