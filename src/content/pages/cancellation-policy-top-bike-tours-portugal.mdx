      # Cancellation Policy – Top Bike Tours Portugal


      Our **Cancellation Policy** ensures clarity and trust when booking our
      **bike tours in Portugal**, both **guided** and **self‑guided**. Below
      you’ll find deadlines, refund percentages, and simple steps to cancel your
      booking by e‑mail.


      ### Quick Summary


      * **Guided Tours**: **≥60 days** → **80% refund** (20% fee); **31–59
      days** → **40% refund**; **≤30 days** → **no refund**.

      * **Self‑Guided Tours**: **≥31 days** → **85% refund** (15% fee); **≤30
      days** → **30% refund**.

      * Cancellations **must be sent by e‑mail**:
      **info@topbiketoursportugal.com**.

      ### Additional Conditions


      * Top Bike Tours Portugal, Unipessoal, Lda. **does not refund services
      booked but not used** by the customer.

      * All cancellations **must be sent in writing by e‑mail** and will be
      handled promptly. Final credit times depend on your payment method/bank.


      ### FAQ


      **1) How do I cancel my booking?**


      E‑mail **info@topbiketoursportugal.com** with your **booking number**,
      **full name**, and **tour start date**.


      **2) Can I change my tour dates instead of cancelling?**


      Date changes are **subject to availability** and may involve **change
      fees**. This does not modify the cancellation policy.


      **3) Can I transfer my booking to someone else?**


      Please e‑mail us the new participant’s details. **Administrative fees may
      apply**; written confirmation is required.


      **4) What if my flight is cancelled or delayed?**


      The policy **still applies**. We recommend having **travel insurance**
      that covers unforeseen events.


      **5) When will I receive my refund?**


      Once approved, we process refunds promptly; final credit times depend on
      the payment provider/bank.
