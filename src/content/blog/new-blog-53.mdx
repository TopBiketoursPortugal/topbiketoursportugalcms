In May 2026, a surprising shift caught the attention of the entire [**Camino de Santiago**](https://topbiketoursportugal.com/blog/overview-of-the-camino-de-santiago/ "Overview of the Camino de Santiago") community.

For the first time (based on shared monthly figures), the **Portuguese Camino attracted more pilgrims than the French Camino**.

## **May 2026 Pilgrim Numbers**

* Portuguese Camino (Central): 16,300 pilgrims
* Portuguese Camino (Coastal): 17,834 pilgrims
* **Total Portuguese Camino**: 34,134 pilgrims
* **French Camino**: 30,701 pilgrims

Difference: **\+3,442 pilgrims on the Portuguese Camino**

## **Is the Portuguese Camino now more popular than the French Camino?**

**Short answer:**

* Yes, in **May 2026 (monthly data)**
* No, **not yet on a yearly basis**

The **Camino Francés still leads in total annual pilgrims**, remaining the most walked route overall.

However, the Portuguese Camino is growing faster and is starting to **dominate in specific months and segments**.

## **Why is the Portuguese Camino becoming more popular?**

The growth of the **Camino Portugués (Portuguese Camino)** is not random, it reflects a shift in how people travel today.

### **1\. Less crowded experience**

The French Camino can be very busy, especially in peak sections.
The Portuguese routes offer **more space and a calmer rhythm**.

### **2\. Authentic local culture**

Pilgrims experience traditional towns, local food, and a stronger connection with community life.

### **3\. Coastal landscapes**

The **Coastal Camino** is a major driver of growth:

* ocean views
* open landscapes
* more emotional and sensory experience

### **4\. Accessibility and flexibility**

Popular starting points like **Porto** make planning easier.
Shorter segments also attract more international travellers.

## **What is the difference between the Central and Coastal Portuguese Camino?**

Understanding this is key to understanding the growth. The differences between the [**Central Camino**](https://topbiketoursportugal.com/tours/santiago-de-compostela-central-way/ "Ride the Historic Portuguese Camino") and the [**Coastal Camino**](https://topbiketoursportugal.com/tours/porto-santiago-compostela-bike-tour-coastal-way/ "Coastal portuguese Way"):

### **Central Portuguese Camino**

* More traditional inland route
* Passes through historic towns
* Slightly quieter than the French Camino

### **Coastal Portuguese Camino**

* Atlantic Ocean views
* More scenic and modern appeal
* Currently one of the **fastest-growing Camino routes**

In May 2026, the **Coastal route (17,834 pilgrims)** even outperformed the Central route.

## **Is this a long-term trend or just a one-month anomaly?**

All available data points toward a **long-term shift, not just a coincidence**.

* The Portuguese Camino has been gaining share every year
* Growth rates are higher than the French Camino
* Coastal routes are expanding faster than traditional ones

The May numbers are likely a **signal, not noise**

## **What does this mean for the future of the Camino de Santiago?**

This shift highlights a deeper transformation in travel behaviour.

### **Key trends shaping the Camino today:**

* Preference for **less crowded routes**
* Demand for **authentic experiences**
* Growth of **active and slow tourism**
* Increasing interest in **Portugal as a starting destination**

The Portuguese Camino is perfectly aligned with all of these trends.

## **Why is Portugal becoming more central to the Camino?**

The data reflects something bigger than route choice.

Portugal is becoming:

* a **gateway to the Camino**
* a **destination, not just a starting point**
* a key player in **sustainable tourism growth**

More pilgrims are choosing to **start earlier (Lisbon or Porto)** instead of joining only in Spain.

## **Will the Portuguese Camino surpass the French Camino overall?**

It’s possible, but not immediate.

### **What we know:**

* The French Camino still leads annually
* The Portuguese Camino is growing faster
* Monthly peaks already favor the Portuguese routes

### **What this suggests:**

A **progressive rebalancing**, not an instant change.

If current growth continues, the Portuguese Camino could eventually **match or exceed the French route in total numbers**.

## **Why this matters for travelers and tour operators**

**For travelers:**

* More choice
* Less crowded alternatives
* New types of experiences

**For tourism businesses (especially in Portugal):**

* Strong growth in demand
* Opportunity to position early
* Increasing international visibility

## **Final Answer: Is the Camino landscape changing?**

**Yes, clearly.** The French Camino remains iconic. But the Portuguese Camino is no longer an alternative. It is becoming a **mainstream choice**. And in months like May 2026, it is already leading.
