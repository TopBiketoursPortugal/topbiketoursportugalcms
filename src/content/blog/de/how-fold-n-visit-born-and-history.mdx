## How a Passion for Cycling Became One of Portugal’s Leading Bike Tour Companies

Top Bike Tours Portugal was not created as a traditional tourism business. It began with a simple idea: exploring Portugal by bike in a more human, authentic, and sustainable way.

Founded in Porto by Sérgio Marques, [Top Bike Tours Portugal](https://topbiketoursportugal.com/) grew naturally from a personal passion for cycling, travel, and storytelling — long before cycling tourism became as popular as it is today.

## From Daily Commutes to Long-Distance Adventures

The story starts with a folding bike. What began as a practical solution for daily commuting quickly turned into something more. Cycling was not just transportation — it became a way to discover roads, landscapes, and people that are often missed when traveling by car.

That curiosity soon evolved into longer journeys. One of the defining moments was a self-supported cycling trip from [Porto to Santiago de Compostela](https://topbiketoursportugal.com/tours/porto-santiago-compostela-bike-tour-coastal-way/), following the Camino de Santiago by bike. The experience proved transformative: challenging, enriching, and deeply connected to local culture and history.

To document these early adventures, Sérgio created a travel and cycling blog, sharing route details, stories, and reflections from the road. The response from readers and fellow cyclists revealed a clear demand for more meaningful cycling experiences in Portugal.

## The Birth of a Cycling Company in Porto

Encouraged by the growing community around cycling travel, the next step was logical: creating a physical space dedicated to bikes and experiences.

The company originally operated under the name *Fold n’ Visit*, offering folding bikes, rentals, and small guided rides around Porto. With time, the focus shifted. Selling bikes became secondary, while guiding people through Portugal by bicycle became the heart of the business.

This shift marked the true beginning of **Top Bike Tours Portugal** as it is known today — a company fully dedicated to [guided](https://topbiketoursportugal.com/tours/tags/guided/) and [self-guided](https://topbiketoursportugal.com/tours/tags/self-guided/) bike tours. City cycling experiences and bike rentals are no longer part of the company’s core business, and we no longer offer these services.

## Focusing on What Matters: Authentic Bike Tours in Portugal

As the company grew, so did its vision. Instead of offering mass tourism products, Top Bike Tours Portugal focused on:

* Small-group and private bike tours
* Carefully designed cycling routes
* Knowledgeable local guides
* Safety, comfort, and quality equipment
* Real connections with local culture, food, and landscapes
* Premium bicycle tours

From Porto’s historic neighborhoods to the Douro Valley vineyards, from coastal routes to inland villages, every tour is designed to show Portugal beyond the obvious highlights.

Today, the company operates tours across several regions, including Porto, the [Douro Valley](https://topbiketoursportugal.com/tours/regions/douro-river/), [Algarve](https://topbiketoursportugal.com/tours/regions/algarve/), [Alentejo](https://topbiketoursportugal.com/tours/regions/alentejo/), and[Camino de Santiago routes](https://topbiketoursportugal.com/tours/regions/santiago-compostela/) — always respecting local communities and promoting sustainable tourism.

## A Local Company with an International Community

Although based in Porto, Top Bike Tours Portugal welcomes cyclists from all over the world. Over the years, thousands of travelers have discovered Portugal through its tours — beginners and experienced cyclists alike.

The company works closely with local partners, accommodations, wineries, restaurants, and luggage transfer services, ensuring that each tour supports the regional economy while maintaining high standards.

Sérgio Marques continues to be directly involved in route design, product development, and quality control, ensuring that the original philosophy of the company remains intact.

## Why the Name Still Matters

Even as the company evolved, its roots were never forgotten. The early days of folding bikes, exploratory rides, and trial-and-error learning shaped the values that still guide Top Bike Tours Portugal today:

* Curiosity and exploration
* Respect for cycling culture
* Honest recommendations
* Continuous improvement

The name remains as a reminder that the best businesses often start from genuine passion rather than business plans.

## Cycling Portugal Today — and Into the Future

Portugal has become one of Europe’s most appealing cycling destinations, thanks to its climate, safety, landscapes, and hospitality. Top Bike Tours Portugal continues to innovate by introducing electric bikes, family-friendly tours, and tailor-made cycling holidays, without losing sight of what truly matters: creating memorable experiences on two wheels.

What began with a single bike and a journey to Santiago is now a carefully built company — still guided by the same enthusiasm for cycling and discovery.
