## **Sintra’s Castle of the Moors: A Journey Through the Mist**

The town of Sintra is home to one of Portugal’s most enchanting landmarks: the Castle of the Moors. Perched high above the town and often shrouded in morning mist, this 9th-century fortress offers panoramic views of forested hills, royal palaces, and the Atlantic Ocean.

### **Cycling Tip: Start Your Climb Early**

If you're planning a bike tour to the Castle of the Moors, aim for an early start. The shaded ascent through Sintra’s dense woodland is peaceful in the morning, and you’ll beat the crowds at the top. This route is featured in many Sintra bike tours, both guided and self-guided.

**Castle of Almourol: A Fortress in the Middle of the Tagus**

Set on a small islet in the Tagus River, the Castle of Almourol looks like something out of a storybook. It’s accessible by a short boat ride and is one of Portugal’s most iconic examples of medieval military architecture.

### **Pair History with Nature**

Many Portugal cycling tours in the central region include a stop here. The surrounding landscapes offer flat, relaxing rides—ideal for those who want a more leisurely pace while taking in the natural scenery and architectural beauty.

## **Alentejo’s Timeless Castles and Vineyards**

The [Alentejo region](https://topbiketoursportugal.com/tours/alentejo-wine-heritage-bicycle-tour/) is known for its rolling hills, whitewashed villages, and well-preserved castles. Riders can pass through towns like Estremoz, Marvão, and Castelo de Vide—each with its own fortress and unique charm.

### **Cycle and Sip**

[Alentejo cycling tours](https://topbiketoursportugal.com/tours/alentejo-wine-heritage-bicycle-tour/) offer the perfect mix of history and wine. After exploring castle walls and medieval streets, you can unwind with a glass of local red at a nearby vineyard.

## **Best Routes for Castle-Lovers on Two Wheels**

If you're dreaming of a cycling itinerary through Portugal’s castles and fortresses, these are some routes to consider:

* Sintra to Cascais: A scenic route that starts with castles and ends by the sea.
* Marvão to Évora: Winding through the heart of Alentejo with historic stops along the way.
* Coimbra to Tomar: Featuring the Convent of Christ, once a Templar stronghold.

These paths are ideal for riders interested in historic bike tours in Portugal, offering a balance of culture, nature, and manageable daily distances.
