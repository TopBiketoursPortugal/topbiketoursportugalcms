## **What is a gravel bike in Portugal?**

A **gravel bike** is a versatile bicycle designed to ride both on paved roads
and unpaved surfaces like dirt tracks, forest roads, and gravel paths, making it
one of the best bike types for exploring Portugal’s diverse landscapes.

## **Why gravel bikes are ideal for Portugal**

Portugal is one of the best destinations in Europe for gravel cycling because of
its natural diversity and extensive network of quiet secondary roads.

**You can ride through:**

- coastal trails in the Algarve and Vicentina Coast
- vineyard roads in the Douro Valley
- cork forests and plains in Alentejo
- mountain gravel tracks in Serra da Estrela

These routes often mix asphalt, gravel, and dirt, which makes gravel bikes the
most practical choice.

## **What defines a gravel bike (key features)**

**Gravel bikes** combine characteristics from road and mountain bikes, creating
a balanced setup for mixed terrain.

### **1\. Wider tyres for comfort and grip**

Most gravel bikes support tyres between 35mm and 50mm, offering stability and
traction on loose surfaces.

### **2\. Relaxed geometry for long distances**

Compared to road bikes, gravel bikes have a more stable and comfortable riding
position, ideal for long tours.

### **3\. Drop handlebars with more control**

Flared handlebars improve handling on rough terrain and descents.

### **4\. Disc brakes for safety**

Hydraulic disc brakes are standard, providing reliable stopping power in all
conditions.

### **5\. Versatile gear range**

**Gravel bikes** typically include wide gearing options (1x or 2x drivetrains),
making climbs easier and descents faster.

## **Gravel bike vs road bike (what’s the difference?)**

The most important difference is where you can ride.

- **Road bike:** best for smooth asphalt and speed
- **Gravel bike:** built for mixed surfaces, including dirt, gravel, and rural
  roads

**Gravel bikes** use wider tyres, more relaxed geometry, and a setup focused on
comfort and stability, while road bikes prioritize speed and aerodynamics.

In Portugal, where routes often combine multiple surfaces in a single ride,
gravel bikes offer much more flexibility.

## **Example: Orbea Terra M20iTeam (Gravel Bike)**

A good example of a modern gravel bike used in Portugal is the **Orbea Terra
M20iTeam**.

### **Key features:**

- Carbon frame for performance and comfort
- Shimano GRX 12-speed drivetrain
- Hydraulic disc brakes
- Designed for both adventure and fast riding

This type of gravel bike is built for long rides across mixed terrain, perfect
for Portuguese landscapes where you transition between paved roads and gravel
sections.

## **Types of gravel riding in Portugal**

Gravel cycling is not just one style. In Portugal, it usually falls into three
categories:

### **1\. Adventure gravel**

Multi-day routes, exploring regions like **Alentejo** or the **Atlantic coast**.

### **2\. Sport / performance gravel**

Faster rides with lighter bikes like the **Orbea Terra**, combining road
efficiency and off-road capability.

### **3\. Bikepacking gravel**

Long-distance travel routes with luggage, often on remote, traffic-free roads.

## **When to choose a gravel bike**

A **gravel bike** is the best choice if:

- you want one bike for everything (road + off-road)
- you want to explore rural Portugal and hidden routes
- you value comfort over pure speed
- you plan multi-day cycling trips

## **Final thoughts**

**Gravel bikes** are the most versatile option for cycling in Portugal. They
allow you to go beyond traditional routes and discover quiet roads, hidden
villages, and landscapes that road bikes simply cannot reach.

If you want freedom, comfort, and the ability to ride anywhere, from Douro
vineyards to Atlantic cliffs, a gravel bike is the right choice.
