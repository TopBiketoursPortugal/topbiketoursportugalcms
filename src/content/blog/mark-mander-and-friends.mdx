Mark Mander is a friend that have done The Way of St. James ( Santiago de
Compostela) bike tour with Fold n Visit last April 15 2018.

group of friends. Actually we were 19 because one Brasilian girl as joined the
gang!

Police in Nova Scotia and forced to retire.

This biketour was a goal for Mark and I am quite sure I will probably meet my
friend again some day!
