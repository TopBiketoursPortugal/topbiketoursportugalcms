Cycling along Portugal’s Atlantic Coast is an incredible way to experience the country’s diverse landscapes, charming fishing villages, and historic sites. The [Silver Coast Atlantic Bike Tour](https://topbiketoursportugal.com/tours/atlantic-coast-to-lisbon-bike-tour/ "Atlantic Coast") is one of the best multi-day cycling tours in Portugal, taking riders from Figueira da Foz to Cascais along scenic coastal routes. This tour offers a mix of beachfront paths, forest trails, and medieval towns, making it a perfect adventure for cycling enthusiasts.

Whether you want to explore surf spots in Nazaré, medieval castles in Óbidos, or the natural beauty of Sintra, this tour provides a unique and unforgettable cycling experience on Portugal’s Atlantic Coast.

## **Why Portugal’s Atlantic Coast is Perfect for Multi-Day Cycling Tours**

The Silver Coast and Sintra region is ideal for cyclists looking for a mix of scenic coastlines, cultural landmarks, and well-paved cycling paths. The terrain is mostly flat with some short climbs, especially near Sintra and Cascais, offering a good balance of challenge and relaxation.

This cycling tour is designed for both self-guided and guided riders, providing flexibility for different cycling styles. Riders can explore historic towns, try traditional Portuguese seafood, and enjoy well-maintained bike lanes along the coast.

## **The Best Multi-Day Cycling Route Along Portugal’s Atlantic Coast**

### **Start in Figueira da Foz – A Classic Portuguese Coastal Town**

The journey begins in Figueira da Foz, known for its wide beaches and vibrant seafront atmosphere. This coastal town is a great starting point, offering an introduction to Portugal’s Atlantic coastline and cycling-friendly roads.

### **Cycling from Figueira da Foz to São Pedro de Moel (65 km)**

The route heads south, passing through Pedrógão and Praia da Vieira, where riders can experience traditional fishing villages and pine forests. The ride is mostly flat with well-paved paths, making for a smooth first day. São Pedro de Moel is a peaceful town surrounded by nature, ideal for an overnight stop.

### **São Pedro de Moel to Nazaré (29 km) – Ride to Portugal’s Surf Capital**

This shorter stretch leads to Nazaré, a town famous for its giant waves and traditional fishing culture. The coastal scenery is spectacular, with cliffside views and ocean breezes. Nazaré’s Praia do Norte is a must-see, home to some of the biggest surfable waves in the world.

### **Cycling from Nazaré to Óbidos (47 km) – Explore a Medieval Town**

Leaving the coastline, cyclists head inland toward Óbidos, one of Portugal’s best-preserved medieval towns. The route passes through São Martinho do Porto, a picturesque bay known for its calm waters and charming village feel. In Óbidos, visitors can explore cobblestone streets, a historic castle, and vibrant local markets.

### **Óbidos to Peniche (35 km) – Coastal Views and Óbidos Lagoon**

The ride from Óbidos back to the coast takes cyclists past Lagoa de Óbidos, a scenic lagoon perfect for a short stop. Peniche, a rugged coastal town, is famous for its dramatic cliffs and seafood restaurants. Cyclists can explore the Berlengas Islands, a nearby nature reserve known for its wildlife and crystal-clear waters.

### **Peniche to Ericeira (68 km) – Stunning Atlantic Views and Surf Culture**

This section follows the coastline south, passing Santa Cruz Beach before reaching Ericeira. This town is a world-famous surf destination, but also a great stop for cyclists due to its laid-back atmosphere and excellent seafood. The ride is slightly longer but rewarding with spectacular views.

### **Ericeira to Sintra (27 km) – Entering Portugal’s UNESCO Heritage Region**

Leaving the coastline, cyclists enter Sintra, a town filled with palaces, castles, and forested hills. This part of the route includes some climbs, but the scenery makes it worthwhile. Highlights include Pena Palace, Castelo dos Mouros, and the lush Sintra-Cascais Natural Park.

### **Final Ride from Sintra to Cascais (40 km) – Coastal Finishing Stretch**

The last stretch of the journey takes cyclists to Cascais, a vibrant seaside town west of Lisbon. The route passes through cliffside roads and ocean viewpoints, offering one last breathtaking look at Portugal’s Atlantic coastline. Cascais is the perfect place to end the cycling adventure, with beautiful beaches, historic streets, and plenty of cafés to relax after the journey.

## **Best Time to Cycle the Atlantic Coast of Portugal**

The [best time for a multi-day cycling tour](https://topbiketoursportugal.com/blog/best-time-to-cycle-in-portugal/) on Portugal’s Atlantic Coast depends on the weather and crowd levels.

* **Spring (March–June):** Ideal temperatures, fewer tourists, and lush landscapes.
* **Autumn (September–November):** Cooler weather, scenic autumn colors, and less crowded cycling routes.
* **Summer (July–August):** Hotter weather but great for coastal cycling with beach stops.
* **Winter (December–February):** Milder temperatures in Ericeira, Sintra, and Cascais, but fewer daylight hours.

## **Self-Guided vs. Guided Cycling Tours in Portugal**

### **Self-Guided Cycling Tours on the Silver Coast**

Self-guided tours allow riders to explore at their own pace while following pre-mapped GPS routes. Luggage transfers and pre-arranged accommodations are included, making the journey hassle-free. This option is best for independent cyclists who prefer flexibility.

### **Guided Bike Tours with Local Support**

For those who prefer a structured experience, guided tours include a local cycling expert, a support van with water and snacks, and organized group meals. This is a great option for riders who want a stress-free experience with extra support.
