title: The El Camino Experience - Biking the coastal route with family and friends
quote: >-
  The Top Bike team was exceptional. Everything was well organized from our
  arrivals, to bike fittings and orientation, to safe rides each day. Our group
  was relatively large (22) so we had 3 guides and a 4th SAG support. Every one
  of these individuals was very professional and personable. I feel that they
  became an integral part of the group and significantly improved every aspect
  of our days together...essentially we became one group, not a group of riders
  and a group of guides. It was great. They were hard working and safety
  oriented. Their days started before ours getting everything organized and
  ended after ours taking care of the bikes, riders, accomodations and more.


  The bikes were very good. I was thinking about bringing my own bike from the
  US but decided to use the bike provided by the tour. This turned out to be a
  very good decision. The staff helped me put on my pedals and saddle as well as
  adjusting the overall configuration to fit my needs. They did the same for the
  other riders in our group.


  I would encourage future riders to be honest in their assessment of their
  climbing ability. The variation in the terrain was one of the best parts of
  the ride, however, there are some climbs that were challenging. Of course
  these can always be walked if necessary. If you are unsure of your climbing
  ability then I recommend getting an e-bike. Several riders in our group did
  this and I think they were all glad that they did.


  I could write a whole review of the scenery, the meals and the accomodations.
  I think we were all blown away by the accomodations, especially the two
  castles and the estate in Spain. It just kept getting better! As to food, I
  had to start cutting back on all of the great foods, especially the pastries.


  As to riding versus walking the El Camino - my primary motivation was to
  accomplish the pilgrimage in a meaningful way. A secondary interest was to do
  it with friends and family. Lastly, I wanted it to be by bike rather than by
  foot based on my age and capabilities. I did wonder if doing it by bike would
  be too easy and not meet my sense for a meaninful pilgrimage. This notion was
  dispelled each and every day that we rode. We definately "earned" the
  completion of the Camino. The Portugese Route by bkie met and exceeded all my
  hopes.


  Finally, we planned this trip as a group of family and friends. The ages
  ranged from 23 to 81 and included couples and singles. The riders came from
  all over the US so there were many questions and accomodations that the Top
  Bike team worked with us on in order to make this a successful trip.
