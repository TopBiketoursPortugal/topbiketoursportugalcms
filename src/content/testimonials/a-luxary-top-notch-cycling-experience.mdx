Thoroughly enjoyed the self-guided bike trip. The "Top Bike Tours Portugal" has provided a luxary top notch cycling experience: the e-bike and MTB's are brand new and from top brands. We also enjoyed very much the choices of hotels along the route, there were two paradors and a resort style accomodation which were very much an unique once-in-a-life time experience. there is an option of adding an extra day in Vigo, which we thought was the right decision, considering our age group.
