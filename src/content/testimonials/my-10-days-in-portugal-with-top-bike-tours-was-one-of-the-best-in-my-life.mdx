My 10 days in Portugal with Top Bike Tours was one of the best in my life— and I am an inveterate traveler. Sergio, João and Alberto paid such great attention to our needs, local experiences and safety! And they all had such warmth and sense of humor. I had an unexpected injury on the bike tour (my fault) and they took wonderful care of me. I will always be grateful to them for introducing me to Portugal’s beautiful scenery and its unique history. But it was their expertise, and more than that, their personal warmth, that made my trip so special. Thank you for the trip of a lifetime!
