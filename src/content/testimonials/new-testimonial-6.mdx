  chosen the unguided option, we were nevertheless guided through and out of the
  city to the coast by Telmo. It is at this point he bid us good luck and we
  were on our own for the next 6 full days of cycling. Guided by the programmed
  GPS provided by TBTP, all we had to do was follow it to the next pre-booked
  hotel where our luggage awaited us. TBTP's choices of hotels was excellent -
  the most amazing hotel was just outside Caldas dos Reis. The GPS was
  deliberately programmed to go through interesting city neighborhoods with
  hints to restaurants and places to rest all the while avoiding many of the
  over-populated routes frequented by walkers. Cycling through some of the
  cities was a little nerve racking because of the traffic. On a few occasions
  we were forced to walk up a hill because of the size of the boulders.

  rain on our first day to Esposende and the odd flat tire, it could not have
  gone any smoother. After a 4 km descent in Vigo, we encountered problems with
  one of the bikes which involved rear brake pad replacement. This was done at a
  local shop which Sérgio took care of immediately over the phone. He was most
  supportive. As a cyclist, a note to TBTP, I recommend fine tuning the bicycles
  before departure particularly stretched chains and brake pads. By the end of
  the 300 km trip, gear skipping, due to stretched chains on all the bikes, had
  become problematic.

  relatively easy with just one long day in Vigo. The scenic coastline was
  absolutely gorgeous with a rustic element. Rolling hills and towns peppered
  the landscape with incredible vegetation. The terrain was varied and perfectly
  suited for a mountain bike or a gravel bike with front shocks - full
  suspension bikes not recommended. For us, the most surprising thing was the
  extent of bike paths and infrastructure supporting the use of bikes in
  Portugal. Portugal is definitely a hidden gem.

  In short, we strongly recommend Top Bike Tours Portugal especially if you are
  new to this sort of adventure. It is basically an all encompassing package
  where the logistics are all taken care of.

  We especially thank Sérgio & Telmo of Top Bike Tours Portugal for an excellent
  holiday.

7-Day "Camino do Santiago" Coastal Route (family of 4)
Sérgio Marques at Top Bike Tours Portugal (TBTP) did an exceptional job organizing our 7-day trip. Telmo, one of Sérgio's team, picked us up at the Porto airport, drove us to the hotel & then to their bike shop (around the corner) where we were outfitted & sized for our bikes. We were then given an excellent 2-hour tour of Porto on foot ending at the Burmester port house on the Douro River where TBTP had organized port-tasting and a tour of the facilities. This was a really nice touch and most unexpected.
The next morning we convened at the bike shop where our Camino began. Having chosen the unguided option, we were nevertheless guided through and out of the city to the coast by Telmo. It is at this point he bid us good luck and we were on our own for the next 6 full days of cycling. Guided by the programmed GPS provided by TBTP, all we had to do was follow it to the next pre-booked hotel where our luggage awaited us. TBTP's choices of hotels was excellent - the most amazing hotel was just outside Caldas dos Reis. The GPS was deliberately programmed to go through interesting city neighborhoods with hints to restaurants and places to rest all the while avoiding many of the over-populated routes frequented by walkers. Cycling through some of the cities was a little nerve racking because of the traffic. On a few occasions we were forced to walk up a hill because of the size of the boulders.
Being an unguided tour, we had to do all bike repairs ourselves. Barring the rain on our first day to Esposende and the odd flat tire, it could not have gone any smoother. After a 4 km descent in Vigo, we encountered problems with one of the bikes which involved rear brake pad replacement. This was done at a local shop which Sérgio took care of immediately over the phone. He was most supportive. As a cyclist, a note to TBTP, I recommend fine tuning the bicycles before departure particularly stretched chains and brake pads. By the end of the 300 km trip, gear skipping, due to stretched chains on all the bikes, had become problematic.
That aside, we can't recommend this trip enough. The coastal route was relatively easy with just one long day in Vigo. The scenic coastline was absolutely gorgeous with a rustic element. Rolling hills and towns peppered the landscape with incredible vegetation. The terrain was varied and perfectly suited for a mountain bike or a gravel bike with front shocks - full suspension bikes not recommended. For us, the most surprising thing was the extent of bike paths and infrastructure supporting the use of bikes in Portugal. Portugal is definitely a hidden gem.
In short, we strongly recommend Top Bike Tours Portugal especially if you are new to this sort of adventure. It is basically an all encompassing package where the logistics are all taken care of.
We especially thank Sérgio & Telmo of Top Bike Tours Portugal for an excellent holiday.
