From start to finish, Top Bike Tours Portugal provided an above and beyond experience! The ride itself -- Camino the French Way -- was a complete blast! An ideal mix of riding on the road, on trails with some single track too. I loved stopping in small villages for coffees throughout the day and the accommodations were top notch. I'm very short so appreciated having the option of a super comfortable, XS e-bike. Our guides were amazing offering just the right mix of professionalism and FUN. This was truly an experience of a lifetime for me. I plan to do another ride with Top Bike Portugal and highly recommend joining any of their tours!
