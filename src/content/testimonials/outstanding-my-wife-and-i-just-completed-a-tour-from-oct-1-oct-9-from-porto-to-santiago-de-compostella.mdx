Outstanding! My wife and I just completed a tour from Oct 1-Oct 9 from Porto to Santiago De Compostella. Almost every aspect surpassed our expectations. To be clear we had never been to Portugal or Spain before so there was some WOW factor but we never got past that...the route, scenery, Bikes surpassed our expectations by a long shot. We consider ourselves pretty hardcore mountain bikers from Colorado and pushed back on E-Mtn bikes but in the end it helped make the trip...we worked hard but when we needed to preserve energy to explore the upcoming overnight stay we did. Allowed us to check out Roman ruins off the tour and other cool sites, without Bike we would have been wasted. We still worked hard and yet felt great finally getting into the pools at the hotels. The Hotels they set us up with were MUCH better than we anticipated. Overall they were a 4 0r 4.5 on a scale of 1-5. Some were a clear 5. No complaints. One example....riding into Baiano, we came around a corner and saw a spectacular castle ahead, the outer fortress spanning a few hundred meters and cool turrets, etc, perched at the edge of the bay. We said we had to go explore it. As it turns out our Hotel was a renovated part of the hotel , so we stayed nb the castle....amazing. I could and will go on later, but Sergio, Telmo, John and others were great. A trip we will tell stories about for years.
