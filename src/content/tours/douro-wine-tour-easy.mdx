  ## **Why Choose a Douro Valley Wine Bicycle Tour**


  The Douro Valley is one of the ultimate wonders of nature. It is the
  culmination of the effort and energy of men and women, who through their
  labour gave shape to an already astonishing landscape. Travelling Douro is
  enjoying its convents and churches, meeting its rustic crafts, tasting the
  genuine cuisine and witnessing the Portuguese folklore traditions.

  Port Wine is undoubtedly the most famous product of this region. Douro comes
  to life during the summer and the beginning of Autumn, with several holidays
  and wine celebrations that happen at this time of the year. The harvest of the
  grapes is most likely the biggest and liveliest event of this region.


  ### **Douro Valley Bike Tour Route Description**:


  * Mostly steep. Up and down route.

  * 100 % paved roads with very low traffic.

  * 7500 meters of cumulative ascents and 7481 meters of cumulative descents


  ### **Day-by-Day Douro Valley Cycling Tour Itinerary:**


  * Day 1: Porto – Pinhão (110 km, van transfer)

  * Day 2: Pinhão loop (55 km)

  * Day 3: Pinhão – Vila Nova de Foz Côa (74 km)

  * Day 4: Vila Nova de Foz Côa – Figueira de Castelo Rodrigo (42 km)

  * Day 5: Figueira de Castelo Rodrigo – Marialva (60 km)

  * Day 6: Marialva – Penedono (28 km, 57 km or 73 km)

  * Day 7: Penedono – Régua (52 km)

  * Day 8: Transfer by train to Porto
