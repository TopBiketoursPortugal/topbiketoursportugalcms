  ## **The French Way**


  Going from Saint Jean Pied de Port in the Pyrenees all the way to Santiago de
  Compostela, the French Way journey is one of the most popular Camino de
  Santiago cycling tours. Its use goes back to the IXth century, when the route
  only extended from Oviedo to Santiago de Compostela.

  Thousands of pilgrims choose this amazing route each year, not only for its
  authenticity, but also for all the amazing places one finds him/herself in,
  while on the road.


  ### **Cycling Route Highlights**


  * 791 km from Saint-Jean-Pied-de-Port to Santiago de Compostela.

  * 12,000 m total elevation gain across mountains, plains, and rolling hills.

  * Pass medieval towns, vineyards, cathedrals, and centuries-old landmarks.

  * Follow the most famous pilgrimage route in Europe.

  * Finish in Santiago de Compostela, a UNESCO World Heritage site.
