  # Biking tour on gentle trails in the heart of Portugal


  ## Coimbra to Aveiro biking loop in Portugal


  Explore the scenic cycling paths along the Mondego, Dão, and Vouga rivers,
  visiting the charming cities of Aveiro, Coimbra, and Viseu. The terrain
  primarily consists of cycling paths and old railway lines, offering a smooth
  and picturesque ride. Enjoy the blend of coastal beauty and inland treasures
  while effortlessly cruising on electric bikes. Discover historic landmarks and
  vibrant culture in each city. This tour promises an unforgettable journey
  through Portugal's stunning landscapes.


  **Itinerary:**


  * Day 1: Transfer from Porto to Coimbra

  * Day 2: Coimbra - Santa Comba Dão (52 km)

  * Day 3: Santa Comba Dão - Viseu (50 km)

  * Day 4: Viseu - São Pedro do Sul (12 km extra + 32km)

  * Day 5: São Pedro do Sul - Sever de Vouga (40 km)

  * Day 6: Sever de Vouga - Aveiro (45 km)

  * Day 7: Departure


  **Itinerary premium option:**


  * Day 1: Transfer from Porto to Coimbra

  * Day 2: Coimbra - Aguieira  (44 km)

  * Day 3: Aguierira - Viseu (63 km)

  * Day 4: Viseu - Vouzela (12 km extra + 34km)

  * Day 5: Vouzela - Sever de Vouga (40 km)

  * Day 6: Sever de Vouga - Aveiro (45 km)

  * Day 7: Departure
