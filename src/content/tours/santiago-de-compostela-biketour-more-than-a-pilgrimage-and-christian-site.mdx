  # **Santiago de Compostela Bike Tour**


  ## **Ride the Historic Portuguese Camino**


  More than a pilgrimage, this bike tour to Santiago de Compostela in Galicia,
  Spain, offers a unique cultural and cycling adventure. Explore the
  UNESCO-listed Portuguese Camino, once traveled by kings and pilgrims, now a
  top cycling route through medieval paths and scenic countryside. Along the
  way, discover traditional Galician markets, local cuisine, and centuries of
  Christian heritage.


  ### **Portuguese Camino Route Overview:**


  Expect a mix of terrains, including challenging climbs and rewarding descents.
  Total elevation gain: **4,435 meters** – ideal for experienced cyclists
  seeking a meaningful and scenic challenge.


  ### **Itinerary - Cycling the portuguese Camino**


  * Day 1: Porto – reception

  * Day 2: Porto – Barcelos (49 km)

  * Day 3: Barcelos – Ponte de Lima (31 km)

  * Day 4: Ponte de Lima – Valença (38 km)

  * Day 5: Valença – Pontevedra (53 km)

  * Day 6: Pontevedra – Padrón (43 km)

  * Day 7: Padrón – Santiago de Compostela (28 km)

  * Day 8: Transfer train to Porto
