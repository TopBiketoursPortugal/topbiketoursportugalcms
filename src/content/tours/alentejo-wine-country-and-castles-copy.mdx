  ## **Explore vineyards, castles, and historical towns in Alentejo by bike**


  Start your journey in Lisbon with a transfer to Marvão, where you'll set up
  your bikes and explore the village. Ride through the scenic Beirã region,
  visiting medieval towns like Castelo de Vide and Crato. Continue cycling
  through the rolling hills to Estremoz, and then to  Monsaraz, enjoying the
  stunning views and local wine cellars. This cycling tour in Alentejo includes
  visits to UNESCO World Heritage sites in Évora, with optional rides to the
  biggest dolmen and a cromlech with over 5000 years. The trip concludes with a
  transfer back to Lisbon.


  ### **Perfect for road bicycles - Alentejo Route Description:**


  * Mostly rolling hills with scenic backroads

  * 85% paved roads (15% smooth unpaved paths)

  * Road bikes follow a fully paved version


  ### **Alentejo Biking Tour Itinerary:**


  * Day 1: Reception in Lisbon and transfer to Marvão

  * Day 2: Marvão - Alter do Chão: 67 km

  * Day 3: Alter do Chão - Monforte: 50 km

  * Day 4: Monforte- Estremoz: 60 km

  * Day 5: Estremoz - Monsaraz: 72 km

  * Day 6: Monsaraz – Évora: 61 km

  * Day 7: Évora Loop – Arraiolos - Évora: 57 km

  * Day 8: Departure


  **Itinerary premium option:**


  * Day 1: Reception in Lisbon and transfer to Marvão

  * Day 2: Marvão - Crato: 55 km

  * Day 3: Crato - Monforte: 63 km

  * Day 6: Monsaraz – Évora: 61 km

  * Day 7: Évora Loop – Arraiolos - Évora: 57 km

  * Day 8: Departure
