  # More of Alentejo Wine country and Castles


  ## Go extra in this route and explore the history, vineyards and the
  extraordinary views


  Start your journey in Lisbon with a transfer to Marvão, where you'll set up
  your bikes and explore the village. Ride through the scenic Beirã region,
  visiting medieval towns like Castelo de Vide and Crato. Continue cycling
  through the rolling hills to Estremoz, and then to  Monsaraz, enjoying the
  stunning views and local wine cellars. The tour includes visits to UNESCO
  World Heritage sites in Évora, with optional rides to the biggest dolmen and a
  cromlech with over 5000 years. The trip concludes with a transfer back to
  Lisbon.


  **Route description:**


  * Mostly rolling hills in safe backroads

  * 100 % of the route is in paved roads  and 15 % is in unpaved and smooth
  paths.

  * If you rent a road bicycle the  route will be 100% in paved roads.


  **Itinerary:**


  * Day 1: Reception in Lisbon and transfer to Marvão

  * Day 2: Marvão - Alter do Chão: 55

  * Day 3: Crato - Monforte: 63 km

  * Day 4: Vila Viçosa – Monsaraz: 55 km

  * Day 5: Monsaraz – Évora: 61 km

  * Day 6: Évora Loop – Arraiolos - Évora: 47 km

  * Day 7: Évora Loop: 36 Km, 44 km or 55 km

  * Day 8: Departure
