  # Camino de Santiago Bike tour


  ## The French Way starting in León, Spain


  Embark on an unforgettable Camino de Santiago bike tour along the French Way
  from **León**. This iconic pilgrimage cycling tour covers historic towns,
  charming villages, and scenic landscapes. **Cycle the Camino** de Santiago
  from **León to Santiago de Compostela**, tracing a route walked by pilgrims
  for centuries. Feel the camaraderie as you pedal through León, Astorga, and
  Ponferrada, immersing yourself in Spanish culture. From rugged mountains to
  lush valleys, the diverse terrain mirrors the stages of personal growth. As
  you cycle onward to Santiago de Compostela, the famed cathedral awaits, a
  symbol of your achievement. Unveil the soul-stirring allure of the Camino de
  Santiago cycling tours on this unforgettable ride.


  ### **French Way Route Description – Camino de Santiago Cycling Tours**


  The Camino de Santiago bike route from León to Santiago de Compostela features
  varied terrain, starting with flat stretches through León’s urban center,
  ascending through rolling hills in Astorga and Ponferrada, steep mountain
  climbs to O Cebreiro, undulating Galician landscapes, and a mix of gentle
  ascents and descents towards Santiago, reflecting the Camino's physical and
  spiritual journey.


  ## Itinerary Options for Your Camino de Santiago Cycling Tour (7 nights / 8
  Days)

  * **Day 6:** Portomartin - Arzúa: (61 Km or 44 km in the premium option)

  * **Day 7:** Arzúa - Santiago de Compostela: 39 Km
