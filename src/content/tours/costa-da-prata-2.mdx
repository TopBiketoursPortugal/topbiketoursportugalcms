  # Coimbra do Lisbon Bike tour- West Coast and Sintra


  ## Cycle through Portugal’s Scenic Silver Coast – From Forest Trails to
  Medieval Towns


  This guided or self-guided cycling holiday in Portugal offers a relaxing
  adventure along the Atlantic Coast. This cycling tour is an immersive and
  amazing route that goes along the coast, where you'll find some of the most
  important villages in Portugal. Here you will have a "mix" of beaches and pine
  forests, creating different sensations during the trip. Exploring the trails
  and their main highlights will give you a sense of fulfillment. Try the best
  dishes in the traditional Portuguese fishing arts, accompanied by wine and
  amazing local desserts. Monuments, like the "Castelo da Pena" and the "Castelo
  de Óbidos", will bring you back centuries into the medieval times. This will
  surely enrich your trip and give you the desire to come back and know more
  about Portugal!


  ### **Cycling route description:**


  * Mostly flat terrain with some short climbs.

  * 30% bike lanes, 30% low-traffic paved roads, and 10% scenic coastal dirt
  trails.


  ### **Itinerary summary :**


  This 9-day bicycle holiday in Portugal covers some of the Silver Coast’s most
  scenic and historic locations. From forest-lined coastal paths to charming
  medieval towns, each day offers something different. The route is designed for
  recreational cyclists seeking bike trips in Portugal that balance culture,
  nature, and relaxed exploration. Here’s what to expect:


  * Day 1: São Pedro de Moel

  * Day 2: São Pedro de Moel – Nazaré (29 Km) – praia do norte- giant waves!

  * Day 3: Nazaré – Óbidos (47 Km) – São Martinho do Porto

  * Day 4: Óbidos – Peniche (35 Km) – Óbidos lagoon

  * Day 5: Peniche – Ericeira (68 Km) – Santa Cruz beach

  * Day 6: Ericeira – Sintra (27 Km)

  * Day 7: Sintra – Cascais (40 Km) – natural park Sintra- Cascais

  * Day 8: Departure


  **Optional extra day:** Figueira da Foz – São Pedro de Moel (65 KM) – Pedrógão
  e Praia da Vieira
