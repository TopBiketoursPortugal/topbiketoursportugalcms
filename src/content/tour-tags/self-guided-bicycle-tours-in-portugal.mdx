      ## What happens if something goes wrong

      The real question, and the one that stops people booking. Four scenarios.

      **You puncture, or something breaks.** Every bike goes out with tools, a pump and spare tubes, and we show you how to use them at the day-one briefing. If that isn't enough, you call. One guest had a flat talked through over WhatsApp and a slipping saddle fixed by a member of our team driving to her hotel the same evening — both on the same self-guided week. We're a Portuguese company operating in Portugal, not a booking agent in another country.

      **You get lost.** Unlikely, and recoverable. You have a GPS unit with the route loaded *and* the same route in Ride With GPS on your phone, with offline maps and turn-by-turn voice — so it works with no signal. Guests also carry paper backups. If all of that fails, call us; we know where you are because we know where the route goes and where your bag is going.

      **You can't finish the day.** It happens — heat, headwind, a bad night's sleep. Call us and we'll arrange the transfer to your hotel. You do not have to ride yourself into the ground to get to your bed.

      **You get ill, or something happens at home.** We'll help with logistics on the ground. For costs, that's what your travel insurance is for — see above.

      **And before any of it:** on day one you get a briefing, a bike fitted to you, and — in Porto — someone rides out of the city with you so your first navigation isn't in traffic. Our guests have also described a phone call every evening to check the day went fine.

      ## Self-guided or guided?

      | | **Self-guided** | **Guided** |
      |---|---|---|
      | Your day | Start when you like, stop where you like | Group pace, set departure |
      | On the road | You and the route | Guide plus support van (min. 8 riders) |
      | Meals | Your choice, every night | Organised group lunches and dinners |
      | If you stop riding | Phone us for a transfer | The van is already there |
      | Insurance | Not included | Liability and personal accident included |
      | Price, Porto to Lisbon | €1,644 | €2,372 |
      | Price, Douro Valley | €1,621 | €2,287 |

      **Self-guided is right for you if** you're comfortable following a GPS route, you'd rather choose your own dinner, you're travelling as a couple or a small group of friends, and you want the price difference in your pocket.

      **Self-guided is the wrong choice if** you want company on the road, you're nervous about navigating in a country whose language you don't speak, you want insurance bundled in, or you're riding a route graded 4/5 or 5/5 without much hill experience. The two that earn that warning are the 8-day [Douro Valley Bike Tour](/tours/bike-tours-douro-valley/) and [Trás-os-Montes](/tours/bike-tours-portugal-douro-wine/) — on both, a van following you is worth the difference.

      **Never done it before? Start with the Douro Valley Wine Cycling Tour.** It is the gentlest week on this page and it exists only as a self-guided trip: seven days, 133 km / 83 mi, graded **Easy (1/5)**, longest day 27 km / 17 mi. Short stages mean you reach each hotel with the afternoon still ahead of you — which is exactly the margin you want the first time a route goes sideways. [See it on the Douro page →](/tours/regions/douro-river/)

      Still deciding? Two longer reads: our [complete guide to self-guided bike tours in Portugal](/blog/self-guided-bike-tours-in-portugal/) and [guided versus self-guided bike tours in Portugal](/blog/guided-vs-self-guided-bike-tours-portugal/).
