      Self-guided means you ride alone, not that you're on your own.

      We book every hotel on the route, move your luggage to the next one each morning, hand you a bike and a GPS already loaded with the day's ride, and pick you up at the airport and at the finish. What you don't get is a guide and a van following you. You set off when you want, stop where you want, and eat where you feel like eating.

      It is the format most of our North American guests choose, and it works from one rider upward — there's no group to fill and no fixed departure date to wait for.

      ## What's included on every self-guided tour

      - Airport transfer to your hotel in central Porto or Lisbon, and a transfer from the finish back again
      - Every hotel booked and paid, breakfast included — you just check in
      - Bike rental
      - A GPS unit with the route loaded, plus the Ride With GPS app with the route and practical notes
      - Daily luggage transfer to the next hotel, so you ride with only what you want on the bike

      You don't need to be your own mechanic. Knowing how to fix a flat makes any self-guided tour easier, and we send you out with tubes, a pump, tools, a lock and lights — but it isn't a requirement.

      ## What self-guided does not include, and what guided adds

      Two differences matter enough to say plainly before you book.

      **There is no support van and no guide.** On a guided tour a guide rides with the group and a van follows it, carrying water and snacks, handling mechanical problems on the road, and giving anyone who's had enough of a climb a lift to the hotel. The guides also organize the group's lunches and dinners. The van runs on guided departures of 8 riders or more.

      **Liability and personal-accident insurance are included on guided tours only.** If you ride self-guided, that cover is not part of the price. We ask every self-guided rider to check their own travel insurance covers cancellation and refund, luggage, medical expenses and repatriation, and death and permanent disability before they fly.

      **Guided Premium** is the guided tour plus 5-star accommodation, upgraded rooms — sea view and premium rooms — and a few experiences we don't put in the itinerary.

      ## Not sure which tour fits?

      Start with the difficulty rating on each tour above. Easy means up to 31 miles (50 km) a day on mostly flat ground with no experience needed. Hard means up to 62 miles (100 km) with climbing over more than half the route. Most people ride somewhere in between — 31 to 47 miles (50 to 75 km) a day with a few hours of climbing.

      If a route looks like more climbing than you want, take the e-bike option. With electric assistance almost every tour on this page rides as Easy, and it is the single most common change our guests make.

      Tell us the dates you're thinking about and how far you'd like to ride each day, and we'll tell you honestly whether the route suits you — including when the answer is a different tour.
