      ### Silver Coast — Porto to Coimbra · 7 days · from €1,434

      The gentlest week we run, and the one we put first-timers on. Graded **easy (1/5)**: five riding days of 40–55 km / 25–34 mi, almost all of it on bike path or quiet road. Out of Porto to Furadouro (47 km / 29 mi), then Aveiro and its canals (45 km / 28 mi), Praia de Mira (40 km / 25 mi), Figueira da Foz (50 km / 31 mi), and inland to finish in the university city of Coimbra (55 km / 34 mi). Sixteen reviews, 4.9.

      *What's hard about it:* very little, and that is the point. The longest day is 55 km on the flat. If you ride thirty-five miles on a rail trail at home without thinking about it, you can do this week.

      ### North of Portugal & Minho · 7 days · from €1,434

      We drive you north to Melgaço on the Spanish border and you ride home down the Minho river and the Green Coast. Valença (43 km / 27 mi, with an optional 6 km hop over the bridge into Tui, Spain), Viana do Castelo (60 km / 37 mi), Ponte de Lima (25 km / 16 mi — the short day, and the best town to have it in), Esposende (50 km / 31 mi), Porto (65 km / 40 mi). Mostly surfaced cycleway, with cobbled lanes, woodland track and short road sections. Graded **easy to moderate (2/5)**. Eleven reviews, 5.0 — our best-scoring northern route.

      *What's hard about it:* the last day is 65 km / 40 mi, and the cobbles are jarring on the wrists. Padded gloves matter more here than on the coast.

      ### Porto to Lisbon — Atlantic Coast · 13 days · from €1,644

      The full traverse, and the trip most of our North American guests book. Thirteen days down the Green Coast and the Silver Coast to Óbidos, Sintra and Cascais — eleven riding days averaging 45 km / 28 mi, longest 68 km / 42 mi, 493 km / 306 mi in total. Graded **easy to moderate (2/5)** despite the length, because the distance is spread thin rather than concentrated. Eighteen reviews, 5.0. From €1,644 self-guided, €2,372 guided. [Full day-by-day itinerary →](/tours/porto-to-lisbon-bike-tour/) · [More on the Lisbon end →](/tours/regions/lisbon/)

      *What's hard about it:* the duration, not the riding. Thirteen mornings of packing a bag is its own kind of effort, and it is the thing people underestimate.

      ## Why Choose Porto, North & Minho?

      After completing your cycling tour, take time to explore the cultural and natural richness of the region. Visit Porto's UNESCO-listed old town, enjoy wine tastings in historic cellars, walk across iconic bridges, or relax along the Douro River. In the Minho region, discover lush green landscapes, riverfront promenades, medieval towns, and friendly local communities that reflect the strong traditions of northern Portugal.

      This region is ideal for cyclists who want:

      - Coastal bike paths, riverside rides, and green countryside
      - A blend of culture, history, gastronomy, and scenic nature
      - Traditional towns like Viana do Castelo, Braga, Guimarães, and Ponte de Lima
      - Tastes of local cuisine and the famous vinho verde wine
      - Easy access from Porto Airport and excellent rail connections

      Porto, North & Minho is perfect for cyclists seeking a cultural journey, a nature-focused adventure, or a complete mix of coastline, history, and authentic Portuguese life.
