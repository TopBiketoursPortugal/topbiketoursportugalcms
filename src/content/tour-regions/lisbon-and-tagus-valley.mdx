## Frequently Asked Questions

**Is Lisbon good for biking?**

Yes, Lisbon is great for biking, especially with an e-bike. While the city has hills, most routes on our tours follow riverside bike paths and scenic urban areas that are easy to ride.

**Where can I cycle in Lisbon?**

Our tours feature iconic Lisbon locations such as the Tagus River waterfront, Belém, Monsanto Forest Park, and quiet urban roads connecting to Coimbra and Porto on longer routes.

**Should I take a bike tour in Lisbon?**

Absolutely. A bike tour in Lisbon lets you explore the city’s vibrant history and coastlines in a fun, active way—ideal for both guided and self-guided riders.

**Are your bike tours in Lisbon suitable for beginners?**

Yes. Most of our Lisbon-region tours are rated as easy to moderate, with support vans and e-bikes available. The Coimbra to Lisbon and Porto to Lisbon routes are flexible for all fitness levels.

**Can I book a private cycling tour from Lisbon?**

Yes, we offer private cycling tours in Lisbon and the surrounding areas, including custom versions of our Coimbra to Lisbon and Silver Coast routes.

**What is the terrain like on the Porto to Lisbon cycling tour?**

This tour follows the Green and Silver Coasts, with mostly flat or rolling terrain, paved paths, and stunning ocean views. Perfect for those who enjoy a longer cycle holiday in Portugal.

**Is it safe to cycle from Coimbra to Lisbon?**

Yes. Our Coimbra to Lisbon cycling tour follows scenic, low-traffic roads and designated cycling trails. Safety support and GPS navigation are included.

**Are support services included on the Lisbon-region tours?**

Yes, all tours from or to Lisbon include luggage transfers, GPS support, mechanical assistance, and access to 24/7 emergency phone support.

**Can I ride an e-bike on your Lisbon tours?**

Absolutely. We provide e-bikes on all Lisbon cycling tours, which are perfect for riders who want help with hills or longer distances.
