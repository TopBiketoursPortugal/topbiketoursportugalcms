One of the most important parts of any cycling holiday is where you stay along
the way. After a rewarding day of riding through coastal landscapes, historic
towns, fishing villages, and Atlantic viewpoints, the right accommodation
transforms the journey from a simple bike tour into a complete travel
experience.

On our
[**Portugal Atlantic Coast Bike Tour**](https://topbiketoursportugal.com/tours/atlantic-coast-to-lisbon-bike-tour/ 'Portugal Atlantic coast bike tour'),
each overnight stop has been carefully selected to reflect the character of the
region you are exploring. From peaceful seaside villages and medieval towns to
world-famous surf destinations and elegant coastal resorts, every hotel allows
you to experience a different side of Portugal's spectacular Silver Coast.

Whether you choose the standard package or the Premium option, your
accommodation provides comfort, excellent locations, and easy access to the
highlights that make this one of the most popular cycling holidays in Portugal.

If you would like to learn more about the route, daily stages, landscapes, and
what makes this journey so special, explore our complete guide to the
[**Portugal Atlantic Coast Bike Tour**](https://topbiketoursportugal.com/blog/portugal-atlantic-coast-bike-tour/ 'Portugal Atlantic Coast Bike Tour: The Ultimate Guide to Discover Portugal’s Silver Coast by Bicycle').
This article provides a detailed overview of the experience, helping you better
understand the destinations, cycling conditions, and unique attractions you will
discover along Portugal's Atlantic coastline.

## **Day 1 - São Pedro de Moel - Between the Atlantic Ocean and the Pine Forest**

**Hotel:**
[**Hotel Mar e Sol**](https://www.hotelmaresol.com/ 'Hotel Mar e Sol') **(4★) or
Superior Room (Premium)**

Your Atlantic Coast cycling holiday begins in São Pedro de Moel, one of the most
picturesque seaside villages on Portugal's Silver Coast. Nestled between the
ocean and extensive pine forests, this peaceful destination immediately immerses
you in the natural beauty that defines much of the west coast.

The atmosphere here feels relaxed and authentic. Atlantic waves crash against
the cliffs while forest trails disappear into scented pine woods, creating a
unique landscape that is both coastal and rural.

Your first overnight stay allows you to settle into the rhythm of the journey
and enjoy the tranquillity of this charming coastal village before the cycling
adventure truly begins.

## **Day 2 - Nazaré - A Night in the Land of Giant Waves**

**Hotel:**
[**Hotel Praia**](https://www.hotelpraia.com/pt/Homepage 'Hotel Praia') **(4★)
or Superior Room (Premium)**

After a short coastal ride, you arrive in Nazaré, Portugal's most famous surfing
destination and home to the largest waves ever surfed.

The town has retained its fishing heritage while gaining worldwide recognition
thanks to the giant waves of Praia do Norte. From the clifftop viewpoints,
visitors can admire the Atlantic Ocean stretching to the horizon and understand
why Nazaré has become a pilgrimage site for surf enthusiasts.

Your accommodation places you at the heart of this vibrant seaside community,
allowing plenty of time to explore the beachfront, enjoy fresh seafood, and
experience the energy that makes Nazaré one of Portugal's most iconic coastal
towns.

## **Day 3 - Óbidos - Sleeping Within Portugal's Medieval Heritage**

**Hotel:**
[**Josefa d'Óbidos**](https://josefadobidoshotel.com/ "Josefa d'Óbidos") **(4★)
or**
[**Real d'Óbidos Hotel**](https://www.hotelrealdobidos.com/en/ "Real d'Óbidos Hotel")
**(4★ Premium)**

Today's journey takes you from the Atlantic coastline to one of Portugal's most
beautiful historic towns.

Surrounded by perfectly preserved walls, Óbidos feels like stepping back several
centuries. Whitewashed houses decorated with colourful flowers line narrow
cobbled streets, while the imposing castle dominates the skyline.

Staying overnight here offers a unique opportunity to experience the village
after most visitors have left. The atmosphere becomes quieter and more intimate,
making it easy to appreciate the remarkable history and charm of this medieval
treasure.

## **Day 4 - Peniche - Surrounded by the Atlantic**

**Hotel:**
[**Star Inn Peniche**](https://www.hotelstarinn.com/peniche/ 'Star Inn Peniche')
**(3★) or** [**MH Peniche**](https://www.mh-hotels.pt/ 'MH Peniche') **(4★
Premium)**

Returning to the coastline, the route leads to Peniche, a town whose identity
has always been linked to the sea.

Built on a rocky peninsula extending into the Atlantic, Peniche is known for its
fishing traditions, dramatic landscapes, and world-famous surf beaches. The
ocean is present in every aspect of daily life here, from the harbour and
seafood restaurants to the powerful waves that attract surfers from around the
world.

Staying in Peniche allows you to experience this maritime atmosphere while
enjoying some of the most spectacular coastal scenery of the Silver Coast.

## **Day 5 - Ericeira - Portugal's Surfing Capital**

**Hotel:**
[**Vila Galé Ericeira**](https://www.vilagale.com/pt-pt/hoteis-portugal/hoteis-costa-de-lisboa/vila-gale-ericeira/ 'Vila Galé Ericeira')
**(4★) or Superior Room (Premium)**

The Atlantic remains your constant companion as you continue south towards
Ericeira.

Originally a traditional fishing village, Ericeira has evolved into one of
Europe's most famous surfing destinations while preserving its authentic
Portuguese character. Whitewashed houses overlook the ocean, fishing boats
remain part of daily life, and excellent seafood restaurants line the town's
streets.

Your accommodation enjoys a privileged position overlooking the coastline,
allowing you to fully appreciate the beauty of the Atlantic at sunset after
another rewarding day in the saddle.

## **Day 6 - Sintra - A Night Among Palaces and Forests**

**Hotel:**
[**Sintra Boutique Hotel**](https://sintraboutiquehotel.com/ 'Sintra Boutique Hotel')
**(4★) or**
[**Tivoli Palácio de Seteais**](https://www.valverdepalacioseteais.com/pt/ 'Tivoli Palácio de Seteais')
**(5★ Premium)**

Leaving the coast behind, today's stage introduces a completely different side
of Portugal.

Sintra is often described as one of the most magical destinations in Europe.
Surrounded by forests and rolling hills, the town is famous for its Romantic
palaces, historic estates, and extraordinary architecture that led UNESCO to
classify the entire cultural landscape as a World Heritage Site.

Spending the night here allows you to experience Sintra beyond the busiest
visiting hours and fully appreciate the unique atmosphere that has inspired
writers, artists, and royalty for centuries.

## **Day 7 - Cascais - An Elegant Finish on the Atlantic Coast**

**Hotel:**
[**Pestana Cascais**](https://www.pestana.com/pt/hotel/pestana-cascais 'Pestana Cascais')
**(4★) or**
[**Hotel Cidadela Cascais**](https://www.pestana.com/pt/hotel/fortress-cascais 'Hotel Cidadela Cascais')
**(5★ Premium)**

Your final cycling day ends in Cascais, one of Portugal's most elegant seaside
destinations.

Once a small fishing village, Cascais became the preferred summer retreat of the
Portuguese royal family and remains one of the country's most sophisticated
coastal towns. Sandy beaches, a charming marina, excellent restaurants, and
beautiful oceanfront promenades create the perfect setting to celebrate the
completion of your journey.

After a week exploring Portugal's Atlantic Coast by bicycle, Cascais offers a
fitting finale before your transfer to Lisbon.

## **Optional Extension - Figueira da Foz - Portugal's Queen of Beaches**

**Hotel:**
[**Hotel Mar e Sol**](https://www.hotelmaresol.com/ 'Hotel Mar e Sol') **(4★) or
Superior Room (Premium)**

Travellers choosing the optional extension begin the adventure in Figueira da
Foz, often referred to as the "Queen of Portugal's Beaches".

This coastal city is known for its wide sandy shoreline, maritime traditions,
and relaxed atmosphere. Along the ride towards São Pedro de Moel, cyclists pass
through traditional fishing communities such as Pedrógão and experience
landscapes where the ocean, dunes, and pine forests merge together.

The day concludes in São Pedro de Moel, providing a peaceful and memorable start
to an unforgettable Atlantic Coast cycling holiday.

## **More Than Just a Place to Sleep**

The accommodation on the **Portugal Atlantic Coast Bike Tour** is far more than
a collection of hotels. Each stay has been chosen to immerse you in the
destination itself, allowing you to experience Portugal's Atlantic coast beyond
the cycling route.

From the giant waves of Nazaré and the medieval charm of Óbidos to the surf
culture of Ericeira, the palaces of Sintra, and the elegance of Cascais, every
overnight stop tells a different chapter of the journey.

Combined with carefully planned cycling routes, coastal scenery, outstanding
gastronomy, and the warm hospitality Portugal is known for, these accommodations
help create one of the most rewarding cycling holidays and biking adventures in
Portugal.
