I have done both guided and self guided bike tours with Top Bike from Porto to Santiago. Sergio and his crew made sure everyone had a great time and went above and beyond to accommodate their guests. If you'd like to go at your own pace and feel comfortable riding solo, self paced is a good option, the GPS was pretty accurate and sergio was available if I had questions via WhatsApp, if you'd like to be with a group and stop for lunch and meet new people, then the group tour is a great option. The accommodations were great in addition to the food and support from Top Bike. All their bikes are new and in perfect condition. I would recommend them to family and friends looking for a top notch company to guide them on their travels...
