We had a lovely bike tour to Compostela de Santiago from Porto. Our hotels were great. Our guides were knowledgeable, thoughtful and accommodating. As our group was large, we had 3 guides, a trail leader, a sweeper and someone to handle the backup van and logistics. They were ALL always busy! I would add that the off-road portion is difficult for beginning mountain bikers. At the trip start, I had a passport difficulty and TOP made the effort for me to join our group ASAP. I appreciated that extra effort.
