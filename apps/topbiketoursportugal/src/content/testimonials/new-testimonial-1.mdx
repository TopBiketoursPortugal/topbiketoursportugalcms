My husband and I have taken many bike tours in the US and Europe, some self-guided. We chose this Porto-to-Santiago tour because our friends were interested in the area and had not gone on bike tours before. All of use are early fifties - mid sixties in age. All of us are regular bike riders, including some mountain bikers.

The best parts of this particular tour experience were our guides (Sergio and João) and the landscapes we pedaled across. There was NO way this could have been a self-guided tour, because the path was sometimes through forest or even narrow, steep streets in neighborhoods.

Our guides always made sure we kept on the path and stayed safe and well-hydrated and fed. They were consistently gracious, tolerant, and accommodating, with a good sense of humor.

Riding along the shore, across beaches, through forest, up and down hills, and through towns kept us engaged. The hotel choices were exquisite, offering a sampling of what rural and urban Spain and Portugal have to offer.

There are several tips I'd like to offer:
1\. This is not an easy bike ride. Yes, there are easy parts and even some easy-ish days, yet you need to train before you go. Make sure you are comfortable in a bike seat for 5+ hours a day and you can negotiate hills. If you have doubts, choose the e-bike option.
2\. If you have not been on a mountain bike before (I had not), it would be good to practice riding one before you go on the trip. Make sure you are comfortable with the heft of the bike and its broad handle bars. Go up and down some hills with loose gravel and/or cobblestones.
3\. When you are assigned your bike on the tour, make sure the seat and handle bars are adjusted for your comfort and safety. Don't be shy!
4\. This tour is destination-focused, so unlike other tours where we stayed in the same hotel for two days while we did local "loops" of rides, this one is all business. There is a schedule. Everyone starts at the same time in the morning. Simply know that that is the case and adjust your expectations.
5\. Also unlike other tours, there are no choices of easy/moderate/difficult rides for the day. We all do the same ride. Again, simply be aware of that.
6\. Unless you are an athlete, or you choose the e-bike option, there will be some hills for which you choose to walk down or up. It's all good. :-)
