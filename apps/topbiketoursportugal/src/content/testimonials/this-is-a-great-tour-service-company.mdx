This is a great tour service company. They take you through spectacular scenery and book elegant hotels. Breakfasts were super. Dinners are either your choice or arranged in famous local eateries. Guides are laidback and very patient. They made lots of stops and always provided plenty of water and snacks of all kinds- savory, salty, sweet, fruits, etc. The countryside in June was amazing but prepare to bike in every terrain possible- tarmac, gravel, dedicated painted trails and forest paths, cobblestone, mud, compact, sandy, board walks, etc. Simply lovely! Highly recommended if you have good knees. I used a Trek Xcaliber 7 mountain bike and loved it.
