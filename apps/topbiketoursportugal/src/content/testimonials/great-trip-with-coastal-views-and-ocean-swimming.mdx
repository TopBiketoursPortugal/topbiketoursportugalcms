Great cycle trip. Porto to Lisbon reduced - guided. The coast was beautiful and I had a couple chances to swim in the ocean along the way. Effect from Hurricane Helen closed some beaches.

It was a small group (8) and two guides. Both guides were relaxed, easygoing competent and able to handle the varied levels of biking ability. Bikes were good. I used standard hybrid, some in the group used e-bikes.

We did not upgrade to premium accommodations and all were very comfortable, very clean and in great locations. It's the first time I've used Top Bike Tours Portugal and would highly recommend them.
