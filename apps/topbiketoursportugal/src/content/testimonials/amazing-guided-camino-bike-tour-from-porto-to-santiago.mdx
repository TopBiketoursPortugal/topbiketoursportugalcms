This was an amazing trip in many ways.
I’ve been on a couple of self guided cycling holidays in the past. This time we went for the guided 7 day, coastal, Camiño route from Porto. My husband and I, both in our 60s we are both keen cyclists (road bikes) . We went for e-bikes after a lot of deliberation. I think this was the right choice for us. The route was extremely varied, beautiful scenery and very interesting. I learned lots of new skills including riding an e-bike !! also riding with a group and varied terrains that we weren’t used to. Every single one of my new cycling buddies were fantastic, super friendly and great fun to be around. The tour was extremely well organised by Sergio, he did a great job of leading, along with support from Miguel; a great team ! We also stayed in some top hotels and tasted some great food. Sergio also took lots of photos, so we had lasting memories of our fantastic trip. I will miss all my new friends (from all over the world dearly). My husband and I are hoping to do another Top Bike tour from Lisbon to Algarve next time. I wouldn’t hesitate to recommend this incredible experience.
