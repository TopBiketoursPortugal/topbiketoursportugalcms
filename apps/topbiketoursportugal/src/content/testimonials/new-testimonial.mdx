This was a fabulous biking adventure! The scenery was spectacular as were the accommodations and the care with which our guides treated us. The terrain was very varied with road and mountain biking intermixed. This kept the rides very interesting everyday. The bikes were great and the camaraderie on this trip made it so much fun. We stayed at a castle one night and a beautiful small hotel with a river running into a pool- amazing. Both of our guides, Sergio and Joao, were so knowledgeable, helpful and fun. I highly recommend this Porto to Santiago de Compostela- coastal path tour!
