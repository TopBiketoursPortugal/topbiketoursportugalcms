Felipe was born in São Paulo, 1998, Brazil but chose Portugal as his new residence in 2022. As a tour guide he is always on his bike and eager to show Portugal to those who come on the tours with him. Passionate about people, he does his best in providing his clients with the best Portugal and Galicia have to offer. Funny enough, tourism wasn't his first choice as a career, he first graduated in Product Design, in order to be able to draw and design cars, two other passions of his. However, thus showed to be a lot harder and not as exciting as he thought, then he changed  his career to nature and adventure tourism and hasn't looked back.On his days off he is always on the bike around Porto, alongside his bouldering activities and boxing.
