Choosing the right **Coastal Camino accommodation** is one of the most important factors for a comfortable and memorable walking experience from Porto to Santiago de Compostela. As explained in our **complete guide to walking the coastal camino from Porto to Santiago**, where you stay at the end of each day has a direct impact on your recovery, comfort, and overall enjoyment of the journey.

Below, we explain exactly **where you stay, why we choose these hotels, and how accommodation works day by day** on the Coastal Camino from Porto to Santiago.

## **Accommodation Philosophy on Our Coastal Camino Tours**

Our accommodation concept is simple: **authentic, comfortable, and strategically located**.

The focus is always on:

* **Comfort and quality sleep**
* **Excellent location** (central or near the sea)
* **Local character**, avoiding large chain hotels

Accommodation is chosen to minimise transfers, ensure smooth daily logistics, and allow you to fully enjoy each destination at the end of your walk.

## **Day 1 - Porto - Starting in the Heart of the City**

**Hotel:** [**Trindade Hotel**](https://www.portotrindadehotel.com/ "Trindade Hotel") **(4★)**

Your journey begins in Porto, a city full of history, character, and energy. Staying at Trindade Hotel places you right in the centre, allowing you to experience Porto before the walk truly starts.

It’s the perfect introduction to the Camino. From here, you can explore the riverside, wander through historic streets, and enjoy the local atmosphere. At the same time, the hotel offers a peaceful and comfortable setting to prepare for the days ahead.

<SnippetsAstroImage image_path="/src/assets/images/trindade-hotel.jpg" image_alt="Trindade Hotel"/>

## **Day 2 - Póvoa de Varzim - A First Connection with the Atlantic**

**Hotel:** [**Grande Hotel da Póvoa**](https://www.grandehoteldapovoa.com/ "Grande Hotel da Póvoa") **(4★)**

After your first walk along the coast, you arrive in Póvoa de Varzim, a town deeply connected to the sea.

Here, your stay reflects that maritime identity. Located close to the ocean, the hotel allows you to feel the Atlantic influence immediately. from the fresh air to the relaxed seaside atmosphere.

It’s a place where the rhythm of the journey begins to settle, combining the effort of the walk with the calm of a coastal evening.

<SnippetsAstroImage image_path="/src/assets/images/grande-hotel-da-povoa.jpg" image_alt="Grande Hotel da Póvoa"/>

## **Day 3 – Esposende – Between River and Ocean**

**Hotel:** [**Suave Mar Hotel**](https://www.suavemar.com/ "Suave Mar Hotel") **(3★)**

Reaching Esposende, the landscape opens up into wide coastal views where the river meets the sea.

The hotel’s location enhances this feeling of space and tranquillity. Surrounded by nature and close to both the river and the Atlantic, it offers a quieter and more relaxed environment.

After a longer walking day, this is where you start to truly disconnect, enjoying the slower pace that defines this part of the coast.

<SnippetsAstroImage image_path="/src/assets/images/suave-mar.jpg" image_alt="Suave Mar Hotel"/>

## **Day 4 – Viana do Castelo – Staying in a Historic Landmark**

**Hotel:** [**Melo Alvim**](https://hotelmeloalvim.com/ "Melo Alvim") **(4★)**

Arriving in Viana do Castelo brings a shift in atmosphere. The town feels more historic, more architectural, and full of character.

Here, your stay becomes part of that story. Set in one of the city’s oldest manor houses, the Melo Alvim blends tradition and comfort in a way that reflects the identity of northern Portugal.

It’s a place where history is present in every detail, while still offering the comfort needed after a demanding walking stage.

<SnippetsAstroImage image_path="/src/assets/images/hotel-melo-alvim.jpg" image_alt="Melo Alvim"/>

## **Day 5 - Vila Praia de Âncora - Slowing the Pace**

**Hotel:** [**Hotel Meira**](https://www.hotelmeira.com/ "Hotel Meira") **(4★)**

As you continue north, the journey becomes quieter and more intimate. Vila Praia de Âncora is a small coastal town with a relaxed and authentic atmosphere.

The hotel mirrors that simplicity. Close to the beach and easy-going in style, it offers a comfortable space to rest and recover.

This is a moment to slow down, enjoy the surroundings, and prepare for the final stages toward the border.

<SnippetsAstroImage image_path="/src/assets/images/hotel-meira.jpg" image_alt="Hotel Meira"/>

## **Day 6 - Caminha - Where Portugal Meets Spain**

**Hotel:** [**Porta do Sol**](https://hotelportadosol.com/ "Porta do Sol") **(4★)**

Caminha is one of the most scenic points of the route, located where the Minho River meets the Atlantic.

Your stay here takes full advantage of that setting. With wide views across the river towards Spain, the hotel highlights the transition that is about to happen.

It’s a place to pause, reflect on the journey so far, and take in the landscape before crossing into Galicia.

<SnippetsAstroImage image_path="/src/assets/images/hotel-porta-do-sol-conference-spa.jpg" image_alt="Porta do Sol"/>

## **Day 7 - Oia - Entering Galicia**

**Hotel:** [**Hotel Glasgow**](https://www.tripadvisor.pt/Hotel_Review-g4714878-d573982-Reviews-Hotel_Glasgow-Viladesuso_Province_of_Pontevedra_Galicia.html "Hotel Glasgow")

Crossing into Spain brings a subtle but noticeable change, in the landscape, the architecture, and the atmosphere.

In Oia, the stay is simple and focused on the essentials. The hotel provides a comfortable base in a town known for its monastery and dramatic ocean views.

This stage feels more raw and authentic, where the connection to the Camino becomes even stronger.

<SnippetsAstroImage image_path="/src/assets/images/hotel-glasgow.jpg" image_alt="Hotel Glasgow"/>

## **Day 8 - Baiona - A Historic and Memorable Finish**

**Hotel:** [**Parador de Baiona**](https://paradores.es/es/parador-de-baiona?utm_source=GoogleMyBusiness&amp;utm_medium=linkgoogle&amp;utm_campaign=paradordebaiona&amp;utm_term=organico&amp;utm_content=ficha "Parador de Baiona") **(4★)**

Your journey ends in Baiona, and the final stay is truly special.

Set inside a historic fortress overlooking the Atlantic, the Parador de Baiona turns the end of the walk into a memorable experience. The setting, surrounded by stone walls and ocean views, reflects both history and achievement.

It’s not just a place to stay, but a destination in itself — the perfect way to conclude your Coastal Camino.

<SnippetsAstroImage image_path="/src/assets/images/parador-de-baiona.jpg" image_alt="Parador de Baiona"/>
