## **What Makes a Walking Holiday Moderate?**

**Moderate walking tours** sit between relaxed beginner routes and more demanding hiking adventures.

They are designed for travellers who want to be active during their holiday while still having time and energy to enjoy the destination.

### **Longer Daily Distances**

Most moderate walking holidays involve:

* Between 15 and 25 km per day
* Five to seven hours of walking
* Consecutive walking days

The distances are achievable for most reasonably fit travellers but require a higher level of endurance than easy routes.

### **Rolling Terrain**

Unlike coastal beginner routes, moderate holidays often include:

* Gentle climbs
* Vineyard tracks
* Forest trails
* Countryside paths
* Hilltop viewpoints

These elevation changes add variety and create some of the most memorable moments of the journey.

### **Greater Sense of Achievement**

Many walkers choose moderate routes because they want a holiday that feels rewarding without becoming exhausting.

These itineraries often provide the perfect combination of challenge, relaxation, culture, and exploration.

## **Who Should Choose a Moderate Walking Holiday?**

### **Is the Douro Valley difficult to walk?**
