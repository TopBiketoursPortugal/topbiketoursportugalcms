Accommodation on the [**Vicentine West Coast in Alentejo Hiking & walking tour**](https://topwalkingtoursportugal.com/tours/hiking-alentejo-vicentine-southeast-portugal-coast/ "Vicentine West Coast in Alentejo Hiking &amp; walking tour") is carefully selected to match the character of each stage of the walk.

Expect a combination of small hotels, rural houses, and farm stays, always chosen for comfort, location, and proximity to the walking route rather than luxury resort standards.

As highlighted in our complete guide to the region, the [**ultimate guide to walking the Costa Vicentina**](https://topwalkingtoursportugal.com/blog/the-ultimate-guide-to-walking-the-costa-vicentina/ "The Ultimate Guide to Walking the Costa Vicentina"), accommodation along this coastline reflects the authenticity of the landscape. It’s simple, welcoming, and deeply connected to local life, rather than polished resort-style stays.

Below is an overview of where you stay each night on this coastal walking holiday.

## **Night 1 - Lisbon**

Your tour begins with one night in Lisbon, allowing a smooth arrival before heading south.

**Hotel:**
4★ [**Hotel Turim Europa**](https://turim-hotels.com/?partner=9486&amp;gad_source=1&amp;gad_campaignid=22418711190&amp;gbraid=0AAAAAoswtd0RpTv_sPhrBUnWF6mxqCv8Y&amp;gclid=CjwKCAjw0dPRBhAPEiwAE5vTTvGmK6eYHgst8OZvqObmJH8vAbipIOV5yobXMrHKi_VO_hj_i0-NkxoCkjIQAvD_BwE "Hotel Turim Europa")

A centrally located city hotel offering comfort and easy access, ideal for resting before the walking stages begin.

<SnippetsAstroImage image_path="/src/assets/images/hotel-turim-europa.jpg" image_alt="Hotel Turim Europa"/>

## **Night 2 - Vila Nova de Milfontes**

After your transfer to Porto Covo and the first coastal walk, you overnight in Vila Nova de Milfontes.

**Hotels:**
3★ [**HS Mil Fontes**](https://dunaparqueresorts-hotels.com/en/hs-milfontes-beach "HS Mil Fontes") *or* [**Casa do Adro**](https://www.casadoadro.com.pt/ "Casa do Adro")

Both options are small, welcoming, and well located, making them a good base to relax after your first full day of walking along the Vicentine Coast.

<SnippetsAstroImage image_path="/src/assets/images/casa-do-adro.jpg" image_alt="Casa do Adro"/>

## **Night 3 - Almograve**

From Vila Nova de Milfontes, the route continues south along coastal paths to Almograve.

**Accommodation:**
[**Monte Longueira**](https://www.montenovolongueira.com/ "Monte Longueira") (Country House)

A traditional rural house surrounded by farmland, offering a quiet and authentic stay typical of the Alentejo countryside.

<SnippetsAstroImage image_path="/src/assets/images/monte-longueira.jpg" image_alt="Monte Longueira"/>

## **Night 4 - Zambujeira do Mar**

This stage finishes in one of the best-known villages on the southwest coast.

**Accommodation:**
[**Herdade do Touril**](https://herdadedotouril.com/ "Herdade do Touril") (Farm Stay)

A comfortable farm stay set in open countryside near the coast, combining rural heritage with modern comfort.

<SnippetsAstroImage image_path="/src/assets/images/herdade-do-touril.jpg" image_alt="Herdade do Touril"/>

## **Night 5 - Azenhas do Mar Area**

Walking continues through wild beaches and unpaved paths toward the Azenhas do Mar area.

**Accommodation:**
[**Casa da Seiceira**](https://casadaseiceira.com/en/discover-casa-da-seiceira/ "Casa da Seiceira")

A traditional whitewashed Alentejo house with the region’s characteristic blue details, located in a calm rural setting.

<SnippetsAstroImage image_path="/src/assets/images/casa-da-seiceira.jpg" image_alt="Casa da Seiceira"/>

## **Night 6 - Odeceixe**

The final walking stage ends in Odeceixe, at the natural border between Alentejo and the Algarve.

**Accommodation:**
[**Casas do Moinho**](https://www.casasdomoinho.com/en/#/home "Casas do Moinho")

A group of rural houses near nature and close to Odeceixe village, offering a relaxed and comfortable base for the final night of the tour.

<SnippetsAstroImage image_path="/src/assets/images/casas-do-moinho.jpg" image_alt="Casas do Moinho"/>

## **What to Expect from the Accommodation**

Accommodation on this **walking tour** focuses on:

* Small, locally run hotels and houses
* Quiet locations close to the route
* Comfort after a day of walking
* Authentic regional character

Luxury resorts are avoided in favor of places that reflect the landscape and lifestyle of Portugal’s southwest coast.

## **In Summary**

Where you stay is an important part of the experience on the [**Vicentine West Coast in Alentejo Hiking & walking tour**](https://topwalkingtoursportugal.com/tours/hiking-alentejo-vicentine-southeast-portugal-coast/ "Vicentine West Coast in Alentejo Hiking &amp; walking tour")**.**

Each night is carefully planned to complement the walking stages, offering a balance between comfort, simplicity, and a genuine connection to the region.
