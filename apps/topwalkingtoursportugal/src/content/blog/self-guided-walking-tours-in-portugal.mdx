## **What Is a Self-Guided Walking Tour?**

A self-guided tour is not a "do-it-yourself" trip where you have to worry about logistics. Instead, it is a curated experience where we handle the details, and you handle the walking. You walk independently, but with the peace of mind that everything is organized behind the scenes.

### **How it Works**

1. **Preparation:** We book your charming accommodations and organize your itinerary.
2. **Navigation:** You receive detailed route notes, high-quality maps, and access to our custom navigation app.
3. **The Walk:** You start your daily walk whenever you choose.
4. **Support:** If you need anything, our local team is just a phone call away.

### **What’s Included**

* **Curated Accommodations:** Hand-picked boutique hotels or rural guesthouses.
* **Luggage Transfers:** Your bags are moved to your next destination while you walk.
* **Digital & Physical Support:** GPS tracks (GPX), mobile apps, and detailed route notes.
* **Breakfast & Logistics:** Daily breakfast and all required transportation/transfers.

## **Why Choose a Self-Guided Tour?**

* **Total Flexibility:** Want to stay an extra hour at a vineyard? Have a late lunch in a seaside village? You control your schedule.
* **Privacy:** Enjoy the solitude of nature or deep, uninterrupted conversation with your walking partner.
* **Walk at Your Own Pace:** Whether you like a brisk morning stride or a slow, meandering afternoon, you move at the speed that feels best.
* **Authentic Experiences:** Without a group, you are more likely to interact with locals, stumble upon hidden gems, and experience the real Portugal.

## **Difficulty Levels Explained**

We categorize our tours to ensure you have the best possible experience:

* **Easy Walks:** Mostly flat terrain, shorter distances (8–12 km), and plenty of time for leisure.
* **Moderate Walks:** Rolling hills, some inclines, and longer daily distances (12–18 km).
* **Challenging Hikes:** Steeper climbs, uneven terrain, and longer days (18–25 km) for experienced walkers.

## **Witch one is the best for you?**

* **Self-guided is best for:** Independent travelers, couples, and those who value privacy and freedom.

* **Guided is best for:** First-time travelers, solo walkers looking to make friends, and those who want in-depth historical commentary from a professional guide.
